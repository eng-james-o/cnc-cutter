# CNC cutter mechanical hardware

Dimensions below are in millimeters. Sources establish component interfaces. The complete cutter has not been run through the Zoo solid kernel or built.

| System | Selected interface and design decision |
|---|---|
| Linear motors | Four STEPPERONLINE 17HS19-2004S1 bipolar motors. 1.8° step, 2 A/phase, 0.59 N·m holding torque, nominal 42 × 42 × 48 body, Ø5 × 24 front shaft, Ø22 register and four M3 holes on a 31 square. CAD uses a 42.3 square clearance envelope and simplified D flat. Verify the driver cooling, configured phase current and available torque at the actual feed and acceleration. Holding torque alone does not size the axis. [Supplier specifications and drawing](https://www.stepperonline.com.au/nema-17-bipolar-59ncm-84oz-in-2a-42x48mm-4-wires-w-1m-cable-connector-17hs19-2004s1.html) |
| Horizontal drives | Two independent 2 mm pitch, 9 mm wide belt loops. 20 teeth give 40 mm/rev. At 614 mm centers each nominal pitch path is 1,268 mm. Use open belt plus clamp allowance or select loop length and slide the idler bridge along the 2040 slots before tightening. The KCL shows both runs and both wraps. Rim teeth and clamp tooth engagement are simplified. [20T drive pulley, Ø5 bore, 20 overall](https://ooznest.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/2GT-Pulley-20-Tooth-5mm-Bore-9mm-Belt-Drawing.pdf) · [20T idler, Ø5 bore, 14 overall](https://ooznest.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/2GT-Toothed-Idler-20-Tooth-5mm-Bore-9mm-Belt-Drawing.pdf) |
| Vertical drives | Two top-driven Ø8 × 600 screws, 8 mm lead. Each has a stepped 5-to-8 coupling, provisional flange and nut envelope, shelf, and lower 8 × 22 × 7 bearing with a shouldered seat. Confirm screw journal finish, end retention and which bearing takes axial thrust. Replace the flange and spring-loaded nut envelope with the purchased anti-backlash assembly before drilling. |
| Linear guides | 550 mm nominal rails, pitch 25, centered 12.5 end distances, Ø3.5 clearance and Ø6 head recess. Model uses an abbreviated MGN12H external block above each rail plus a separate 8 mm drilled shim. This preserves the existing carriage datum with a nominal 13 mm assembled guide. Confirm the actual rail end pattern, block engagement, preload and supplier slot adapter. [HIWIN guideway catalog, MGN series](https://www.hiwin.com/wp-content/uploads/Linear_Guideway-E-1.pdf) |
| Homing and travel limits | Eight Omron SS-5GL hinge-lever positions. Base plate edges actuate the horizontal switches. Carrier edges actuate the vertical switches. A removable cam on the underside of the disc operates the ninth switch. Bodies use the 19.8 length, 6.4 thickness, Ø2.35 mounting holes at 9.5 spacing and 14.5 lever. Position at the nominal 8.8 operating datum, then adjust and measure electrical trip and stopping distance before setting firmware travel. [Omron SS family dimensional drawing](https://www.nexinstrument.com/assets/images/pdf/SS-5GL.pdf) |
| Rotary indexer | Fifth motor uses the same NEMA17 envelope. Provision for a 30:1–60:1 worm reducer, motor adapter, keyed Ø20 spindle, separate thrust bearing, tapped drive flange, bolted table and home flag. The housing, adapter holes, bearing and shaft interfaces are provisional until a matched reducer is selected. Internal teeth and anti-backlash features are omitted. |
| Wire and take-up | One fixed left termination and one moving right termination. A 0.4 mm wire passes through bored ceramic guides. The right tail follows a 90° redirect then connects via a short insulating cord to the pull strip. Reel and guide share the same carrier. Two stops bound the strip tab. A 15 mm aligned cold payout leaves room to retract with heating. The largest guide offset adds 78.46 mm. A nominal 8–10 N spring with 120 mm usable stroke still needs a force curve and supplier drawing. Redirect material, tail flex life, clamping and hot insulation need physical testing. The 1.9 m aligned current-injection interval is shown by the two clamps. |
| Workholding and control | Separate stationary supports and a removable rotary spacer share the foam datum. Four slotted edge jaws locate the stationary 300 mm-deep blank. Select a sacrificial fixture for the rotary table to suit the actual stock. Controller is an external mounting envelope including ears and the Ø22.5 E-stop panel opening. Cable routing, cable carriers, terminals, guards and internal electrical assemblies remain to be detailed. |

## Comparison with working machines

[RC Keith’s four-axis hot-wire cutter](https://rckeith.co.uk/cnc-hot-wire-foam-cutter/) uses independent towers, stepper-driven screws and limit inputs. Its [USB build parts list](https://rckeith.co.uk/usb-hot-wire-cnc-foam-cutter-parts-list/) lists four NEMA 17 motors. This supports the independent linear-axis arrangement. Its guide and drive choices differ from this hybrid belt/screw machine.

[How To Mechatronics’ foam cutter, summarized by Arduino](https://blog.arduino.cc/2020/02/08/make-shapes-out-of-foam-with-this-arduino-controlled-hot-wire-cutter/) combines aluminum extrusions, printed parts and three stepper motors. It provides a useful construction comparison for printed mounts. Neither build validates this machine's two-meter span, spring stroke, 400 mm travel or loaded rotary stage.

## Fabrication and remaining fit checks

The motor faces are 6 mm thick with Ø22.5 register clearance and Ø3.4 bolt clearances. The horizontal motor bridge has a 130 mm rail-center span. Print one bridge and check pilot fit, screw engagement, creep, motor-case temperature and alignment under belt load before duplicating it. The same plate outlines can be machined in aluminum. Fit-test the idler shoulder and washers against the inner races. Ensure that screw tips clear the raceways and motor internals.

Use the joint-by-joint [fastener schedule](fasteners.csv) with the root BOM. A thread is represented by a pilot bore or nominal mating bore. Use the selected extrusion manufacturer's slot nuts and verify section lips and end-tap cores. Solid rectangular stock envelopes hide the commercial T-slots. Rotary blank-specific clamps, bench holes and supplier-specific attachment details still require the final components. Offline evaluation does not measure stopping distance, collisions, sag, loaded torque or printed-part durability.
