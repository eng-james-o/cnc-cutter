# CNC Hot-Wire Cutter Design Notes

Project memory for Zoo Design Studio's AI agent. Updated 2026-11-06.
Keep this file beside `main.kcl` and update it after substantive design changes.

- **Design basis:** Read `../ARCHITECTURE.md`, `../BOM.csv` and `README.md` before changing the model. The current baseline is architecture v0.2 and preliminary CAD layout v0.1. Treat dimensions explicitly described as provisional as assumptions; preserve architecture requirements unless the user changes them. If sources disagree, record the discrepancy rather than silently changing the requirements.
- **Purpose:** Independent dual-tower, four-linear-axis hot-wire cutter for tapered/swept wing and fuselage sections, with a fifth vertical rotary B axis initially intended for indexed cuts. EPS/XPS are the initial materials. This remains a concept assembly requiring mechanical detailing.
- **Coordinate convention:** World X runs across the wire span, left tower at negative X and right tower at positive X. World Y is horizontal cutting travel; world Z is up. The origin is centered in the bed plan, with Z = 0 at the underside of the 2040 bed members; underside braces extend down to Z = -8 mm.
- **Controller-axis mapping:** X1/X2 translate their complete towers along world Y. Y1/Y2 move the guide carriers along world Z. B rotates the workholding fixture about world Z. Preserve this distinction between controller names and CAD axes.
- **Entry points:** `main.kcl` contains the structural assembly and rail envelopes. `layout.kcl` imports that assembly and adds vertical blocks/carriers, guide arms, ceramic guide envelopes, tensioner reservation, separate workholding and foam references. Use the structural entry point when exporting frame geometry.
- **Shared files:** `parameters.kcl` owns shared driving dimensions, axis poses and colors. `parts.kcl` provides rectangular stock, triangular gusset and disc helpers. Reuse these before introducing duplicate helpers; keep physical members as separate named bodies.
- **KCL conventions:** All current KCL files declare KCL 3.0 with millimeters as the default length unit. Preserve consistent versions across imports, explicit angle units and deliberate global transforms. Helper sketches use dimension-driven fixed coordinates. Sketches inside custom functions are edited through KCL and are not point-and-click editable in Design Studio.
- **Wire-guide spacing:** Ceramic guide center planes are at X = -1000 and +1000 mm, giving 2000 mm separation. This dimension is between guide planes, not tower centers, bed edges or wire cut ends.
- **Foam capacity:** The architecture specifies a centered 1600 mm foam span, leaving nominal 200 mm each side to the guide planes. The layout blank is 1600 × 300 × 300 mm, extending from Z = 140 to 440 mm; its 300 mm depth/height are reference assumptions. A future 2000 mm foam blank requires increased guide spacing and renewed mechanical/thermal checks.
- **Linear travel and displayed pose:** Each linear axis has 400 mm usable travel. `x1Position` and `x2Position` range from -200 to +200 mm; `y1Position` and `y2Position` range from 0 to 400 mm above the minimum guide height. Defaults are 0, 0, 200 and 200 mm respectively. Pose parameters do not constitute motion or collision simulation.
- **Bed:** Braced 2040 aluminium stock envelopes form four 700 mm bed beams, four 2090 mm span ties and four 110 mm tower-bed end ties. The 40 mm dimension is vertical. Nominal bed-stock footprint is 2390 × 700 mm; moving base plates increase overall structural width to 2440 mm, excluding the outboard tensioner reservation and future hardware.
- **Moving towers:** Tower centers are X = ±1120 mm, 120 mm outward of the guide planes. Each tower has a 200 × 200 × 8 mm moving base plate, two 620 mm uprights and a 150 mm cap. Uprights use the 40 mm dimension across the wire span; caps are 20 mm high. The tower feet are at Z = 69 mm and caps reach Z = 709 mm.
- **Bracing:** Two 20 × 4 mm underside steel straps form an X; their separate levels require 4 mm end spacers for the lower strap. Each tower has an unperforated triangular side-web envelope, 200 mm base × 180 mm high × 6 mm thick. Strap attachments, gusset holes and joint hardware remain undefined.
- **Linear guides:** Eight 550 mm MGN12H rail envelopes implement two rails per axis. Paired rail centers are 130 mm apart. The model uses eight blocks minimum: four horizontal blocks in `main.kcl` and four vertical blocks added by `layout.kcl`. Rail/block sections, stack heights and 45 mm block lengths are provisional; verify against selected supplier drawings before detailing mounts.
- **Guide-carrier heights:** Guide centers range from Z = 189 to 589 mm, with both at Z = 389 mm in the default pose. Preserve full-stroke block containment and positive guide-arm reach when changing rail stacks, plate thicknesses or tower offsets.
- **Drive provisions:** Architecture candidates are GT2, 2 mm pitch, 9 mm belts and 20-tooth pulleys for horizontal axes; T8×8 screws for vertical axes; four NEMA17 linear motors. Drives, bearing supports, anti-backlash nuts, belt paths, endstops and their mounts are not modeled. Check motor packaging, tool access and travel margins when adding them.
- **Wire and tension:** Architecture wire is 0.40 mm NiCr80 with preliminary 8–10 N tension. Maximum nominal skewed guide-to-guide path is approximately 2078.46 mm; the initial cut-wire allowance is about 2250 mm. These are architecture calculations, not CAD-measured or experimentally verified results.
- **Thermal take-up:** Reserve 100–120 mm usable take-up, plus stops and attachment allowances. The current orange 120 × 60 × 40 mm outboard body is a space reservation, not an operating spring or validated housing. Constant-force spring, pulley/cord routing and attachments remain to be designed.
- **Electrical isolation at interfaces:** Ceramic guide discs are location envelopes without bores. Detail insulated guides and current clamps so the spring, frame, bearings and pulley do not form part of the wire-current path. The architecture uses a separate isolated heater supply and external power stage; keep its 48 V supply away from RAMPS power inputs.
- **Separate support and rotary fixture:** Green foam supports and the central rotary pedestal are reference volumes requiring a separately supported bench/fixture. The rotary plate is Ø250 × 12 mm, with an 8 mm fixture spacer. Bearing support, reducer, motor, load rating, home sensor and B-axis motion remain undefined.
- **Rotary clearance:** Stationary wing supports must be repositioned or removed for rotation. A 1600 × 300 mm blank has approximately 1628 mm planar swept diameter. Check workpiece, wire, towers, carriers, support, bench and room clearance throughout the intended indexed angles; the 700 mm bed depth does not establish rotary clearance.
- **Colors:** Light grey indicates aluminium stock envelopes, dark grey steel/hardware envelopes, blue plates/blocks/rotary plate, beige ceramic guides and foam references, green separate supports, and orange the tensioner keep-out. Inspect body names and entry points to distinguish reference volumes from intended parts.
- **Cut list:** `frame_cut_list.csv` is regenerated by `python cad/check_dimensions.py` from the repository root. Default 2040 total is 14.38 m before kerf and waste. Diagonal lengths are nominal joint-centerline distances; fastening overlap and end details still require allowances.
- **Mass and CG analysis:** No cutter mass or CG result has been established. Solid 20 × 40 mm envelopes do not represent actual T-slot material area and must not be multiplied by aluminium density as if they were finished extrusions. Use supplier mass per meter for extrusion lengths, actual plate geometry/material and verified component masses; identify omitted motors, wiring, fasteners, heater hardware and fixtures.
- **Future mass reporting:** If a cutter `MASS_CG.md` is added, record the assembly revision, included bodies, world-coordinate reference, displayed axis pose, material assumptions, supplier masses and exclusions. Separate geometric volume/centroid output from the installed mass budget. Moving towers/carriers change CG with pose. Exclude foam and keep-out bodies from machine mass; report workpiece/support contributions separately when assessing bench loading and stability. The supplied drone mass report is a formatting example only; its values do not apply here.
- **Validation status:** The initial CAD files passed parsing/formatting and lint checks with Zoo CLI 0.2.209. The dimension script checked nominal spacing, rail fit, plate coverage, full-stroke block containment and guide-arm reach. Initial geometry execution, visual inspection and STEP export were not verified because Zoo authentication was unavailable. Do not describe those checks as completed until they are actually run.

Record verified results and remaining gaps here.
